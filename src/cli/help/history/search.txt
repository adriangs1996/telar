telar history search: Search commands by text

Usage: telar history search QUERY [filters] [--after ID | --offset N] [--fuzzy] [--distinct] [--json | --format TEMPLATE] [--socket PATH]

Arguments:
  QUERY            The text to find; at most 1024 bytes. With --fuzzy, its
                   characters in order.

Results: as `history list`, which explains the options. Exit 0.
