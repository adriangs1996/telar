telar history stats: Count commands and show the most frequent ones over a period

Usage: telar history stats [--period today|week|month|year|all] [--ngram-size N] [--cwd | --workspace PATH | --pane ID] [--socket PATH]

Arguments:
  --period P       `today` (since local midnight), `week`, `month` or `year`
                   (the last 7, 30 or 365 days) or `all` (default).
  --ngram-size N   Count sequences of N commands run one after another, 1 to 10
                   (default 1), shown joined by ` ; `.

Results: `commands: N`, `unique: N` and the top 10 commands or sequences with
their counts. Only the scope and the period filter; --failed, --author and
--limit are ignored here. Exit 0.
